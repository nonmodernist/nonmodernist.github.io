oliver reed steals a schoolteacher (candice bergen) because he wants to learn to read or something, only to have her wealthy husband (a slimy gene hackman) hunt down and pick off reed's crew a handful at a time, in this western that's equal parts peckinpah rip-off and bodice ripper.

the kidnapping, it turns out, rescues bergen from nights of marital rape at the hands of hackman, whose idea of vacation fun is branding a sex worker with his cigar. this genteel brutality is intercut with reed's gang slaughtering cattle to eat. the film trusts us to know who is the real monster, and to tell the difference between the several assaults reed rescues bergen from and the push-pull of her seduction at his hands.

it's a neat trick, actually, using the plot beats of the era's romance novels to thwart (and then, finally, horribly, confirm) the cynical nastiness of this era's westerns.
