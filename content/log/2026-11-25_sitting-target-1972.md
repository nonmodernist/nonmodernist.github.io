this movie rules. oliver reed is settling in for a long prison stint when his wife visits to tells him she's pregnant with someone else's baby. consumed with the need to kill her for the betrayal, reed escapes from prison with his boyfriend ian mcshane, who spends a lot of the movie having sex with various women while oliver reed looks pained and forlorn nearby. "you'll have to excuse my friend," mcshane says to one woman, "he's feeling a bit queer."

it's an absolute miracle of an oliver reed performance. he's somehow brutal and tender in the exact same moments. he makes ian mcshane look like a twink just by existing. he does a bunch of stunts and works those big beautiful melancholy eyes and buries his face into an empty fur coat like it's the bush of the most intoxicating woman in the world and he's sad about how badly he wants it.

my god!!!
